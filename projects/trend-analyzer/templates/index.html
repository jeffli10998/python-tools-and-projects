Standardization (Z-Score)

Rescales data to have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1. This is ideal when your series have different scales (e.g., an index at 20,000 vs a stock at $100) but you want to compare their relative volatility and movements.

Min-Max Scaling

Squashes all data points into a fixed range of 0 to 1. This is best for seeing which series reached their relative peak or trough at the same time, regardless of how much they actually moved in absolute terms.

Pearson Correlation

A value from -1 to +1 measuring how closely two series move together.
• 1.0: Perfect positive relationship.
• 0.0: No linear relationship.
• -1.0: Perfect inverse relationship.
