gba-emulator-nextjs/
├── app/ (or pages/)
│   ├── layout.js (or _app.js and _document.js in pages/)
│   ├── page.js (this will be your main page, e.g., for the emulator)
│   └── ... (other pages or route groups)
├── components/
│   ├── EmulatorDisplay.js       # Component to host the GBA emulator
│   ├── GameControls.js          # Component for play, pause, load ROM, etc.
│   ├── CheatInterface.js        # Component for modifying game characteristics
│   └── Layout.js                # Optional: Main layout for your app
├── public/
│   └── ... (static assets like images, and potentially GBA BIOS if needed by emulator)
├── lib/ or utils/
│   └── emulatorService.js       # (Optional) For abstracting emulator interactions
└── ... (other Next.JS files and folders: next.config.js, package.json, etc.)
